Every business runs on a handful of workflows: a lead comes in, someone
follows up, a piece of content goes out, a report gets made. Most of those
are held together by tools you rent.

## The test

Ask one question of every tool in the chain: *if it disappeared tomorrow,
could we leave in an afternoon and take our data with us?*

If the answer is no, you don't own that part of your business. You lease it.

## What owning looks like

- **Your data lives in files you can open.** A SQLite database, a folder of
  Markdown, a spreadsheet you can export. Not a dashboard.
- **Your workflows run on infrastructure you can move.** A container on any
  host, not a proprietary runtime.
- **Your integrations use open contracts.** Webhooks with signatures, REST
  with API keys, pull requests for content. Boring on purpose.

## The trade

Owning costs a little more up front and a little attention each month. In
exchange, nobody can change your pricing, sunset your feature, or lock your
data. That trade is worth it for anything that touches revenue.
